Moringa oil for your face: how much, when, and for whom

Two or three drops at night is usually enough. Here is why, and who should skip it.

Moringa is the drumstick tree, sahjan, that grows across much of India. Its seeds give a light oil. For our oil, the seeds are dried in the sun before they are pressed, and nothing else is added.

What it does

Like most plant oils, moringa seed oil is an emollient: it softens the surface of the skin and helps it feel less dry. There is no good evidence that it changes wrinkles or elasticity, so we do not suggest it does.

How much and when

Two or three drops is enough for face and neck. Warm them between your palms and press them on after your mist or gel at night. Night is when an oil has the most time to work.

Who should go easy

If your skin is oily or breaks out easily, try a single drop on a few nights first, or stay with the aloe gel. As with anything new, patch test on your inner arm.
