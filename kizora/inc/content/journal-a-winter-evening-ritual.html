A winter evening ritual for dry skin and heels

When the air turns dry, add two small steps to your evening.

In north Indian winters, skin that was fine in October can feel tight by December. Heels crack, lips peel. A good evening ritual helps, and it does not need many products.

Keep the base simple

Cleanse with a mild bar, mist with rose water, then press two or three drops of moringa oil onto your face. If you prefer a gel, use the aloe gel with vitamin E.

Add lips and feet

Finish with a rich lip balm, and massage the ghee foot butter into your heels before putting on cotton socks. Our founder tells us older customers often reorder it for dry, cracked heels.

When to see a doctor

Cracks that are deep, bleed or hurt need medical care, not just a foot butter.
