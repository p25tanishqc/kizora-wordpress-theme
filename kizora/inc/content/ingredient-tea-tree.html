Plant waters

Tea tree leaf water

The water left after distilling tea tree leaves (a hydrosol), not the concentrated tea tree oil.

Suits: Oily and combination skin that wants a light refresh.

Traditional use

Tea tree is an Australian plant; its oil became popular worldwide for oily skin.

What the evidence says

Most research is on tea tree OIL, which has antimicrobial properties in laboratory studies and some clinical use for mild acne. A leaf water contains far smaller amounts of those compounds, so oil studies cannot be borrowed for this product.

How it works on skin

A fresh, herbal mist that feels clarifying on oily skin.

Who should be careful

Avoid eyes; stop if irritation occurs. It is not a treatment for acne.
