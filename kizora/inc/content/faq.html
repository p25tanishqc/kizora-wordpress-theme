Where are Kizora products made?+

In a small unit near Kanha National Park, Madhya Pradesh, run by our botanist partner with people from nearby villages. Our founder works from Gurugram.

Are your products Ayurvedic or certified organic?+

Not today. We make clean, small-batch skincare. Our Ayurvedic certification is in process and we will update this answer when it is granted.
