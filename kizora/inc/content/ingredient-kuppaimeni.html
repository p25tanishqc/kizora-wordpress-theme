Kuppaimeni (Indian nettle)

Indian nettle

A common wayside plant, called kuppaimeni in Tamil.

Suits: Everyday body cleansing for those who like traditional ingredients.

Traditional use

Used in Siddha and household remedies, including on skin.

What the evidence says

Scientific reviews describe its traditional uses and laboratory findings; there are no published cosmetic studies we could find. Present it as a traditional ingredient.

How it works on skin

Part of a mild cold-process soap with coconut and castor oils.

Who should be careful

Patch test; describe tradition, not results.
