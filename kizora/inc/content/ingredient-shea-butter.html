Shea butter

A plant butter from the nut of the African shea tree.

Suits: Dry skin, heels, lips; adds creaminess to soaps.

Traditional use

Long used in West Africa on skin and hair.

What the evidence says

A widely used emollient in cosmetics.

How it works on skin

Softens and helps skin feel less dry.

Who should be careful

Rare nut-related sensitivity.
