Process

Slow is the point.

Everything we sell is made at one small unit near Kanha National Park in Madhya Pradesh, run by our founder's partner, who is trained in botany, together with people from nearby villages.

  1. 01

    Grown and gathered

    Plants are grown without chemical fertilisers, according to our partner, and sourced from the area around the unit where possible.

  2. 02

    Made by hand, in small batches

    Wooden and stone tools and very few machines. Batches are small so they can be checked by hand.

  3. 03

    Oils pressed slowly

    Oils are extracted slowly. For our moringa oil, the seeds are dried in the sun first.

  4. 04

    Soaps left to cure

    Our natural soaps rest for 21 to 28 days before they are wrapped, so they harden and last longer.

  5. 05

    Checked and packed at source

    Our partner leads quality control and documentation at the unit before products travel to you.

Our products are not sold as Ayurvedic or certified organic today. Our Ayurvedic certification is in process.
