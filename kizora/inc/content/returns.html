Returns, refunds and cancellations

Skincare is personal and, once opened, cannot be resold. So we accept returns when something has gone wrong on our side: the product arrived damaged, leaked, or you received the wrong item.

What to do

Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and a photo of the product and its packaging, within the time stated in our full policy. We will replace it or refund you.

Cancellations

You can cancel an order before it is dispatched by messaging us.

For anything not covered here, contact us.
