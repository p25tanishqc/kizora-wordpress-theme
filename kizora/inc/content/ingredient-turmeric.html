Turmeric (haldi)

Haldi

The dried, ground root of the turmeric plant.

Suits: Dull-looking skin as part of a weekly ritual.

Traditional use

Central to ubtan and the haldi ceremony before Indian weddings.

What the evidence says

A systematic review found early clinical evidence for some skin uses of turmeric, with study quality still limited. In a rinse-off bar its contact time is short, so describe it as traditional rather than as a treatment.

How it works on skin

In an ubtan bar it adds colour and a traditional feel; chickpea flour gives gentle polish.

Who should be careful

Can stain light fabrics and nails; patch test.
