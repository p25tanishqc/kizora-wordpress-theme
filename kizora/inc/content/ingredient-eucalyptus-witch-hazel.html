Eucalyptus water and witch hazel

Nilgiri

Two plant waters in the Eucalyptus Soothing Mist: eucalyptus for a cool, fresh smell, and witch hazel, traditionally used as a mild astringent.

Suits: Hot, humid days; post-workout refresh.

Traditional use

Witch hazel water is a long-standing toner ingredient.

Limited skin-specific evidence; describe the cooling feel, not medical effects.

How it works on skin

Who should be careful

Strong scents can bother sensitive skin; avoid eyes.
