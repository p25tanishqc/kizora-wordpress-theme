Desi cow ghee

Ghee

Clarified butter made from cow's milk.

Suits: Dry, rough heels and dry lips.

Traditional use

Applied to cracked heels and dry lips in many Indian homes.

What the evidence says

A rich fat that acts as an emollient and seals in moisture; clinical cosmetic studies are scarce. Our founder reports that older customers reorder the ghee foot butter for cracked heels.

How it works on skin

Forms a soft, protective layer that slows water loss.

Who should be careful

Animal-derived: products with ghee are not vegan.
