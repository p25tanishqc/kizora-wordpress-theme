Why our soaps take a month

How cold-process soap is made, and why we wait 21 to 28 days before packing it.

Our natural soaps are made the slow way, in small batches at our partner's unit near Kanha National Park in Madhya Pradesh.

What "cold process" means

In cold-process soap making, oils such as coconut and castor are mixed with lye at low temperatures, poured into moulds and cut into bars. Freshly cut bars still hold a lot of water.

Why we wait

So we leave the bars to cure for 21 to 28 days before they are checked and packed. As the bars cure, excess water gradually evaporates, helping them become firmer and last longer in use.

How to make a bar last

Keep it on a soap dish that drains, out of the stream of water. A dry bar lasts much longer than one sitting in a puddle.

Our current soaps include Kuppaimeni (Indian nettle), Hibiscus & Rose and Tomato Extract. Each product page explains its key ingredients and how to use it.
