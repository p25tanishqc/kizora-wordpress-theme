Our story

Made slowly, near Kanha.

Kizora began with two people. Bikash spent more than twenty years in fashion and beauty, with names like Raghavendra Rathore, Ritu Kumar and Nykaa. A friend of his, trained in botany, had set up a small unit near Kanha National Park in Madhya Pradesh to make products with the villages around it.

Today that unit makes what we sell. Bikash looks after the brand from Gurugram; his partner looks after the making and the quality.

No compromise on quality. Follow every rule and keep every document. Build around a strong idea, and build it to last.

The three rules our founder, Bikash, works by

Slow, on purpose

Few machines, wooden and stone tools, oils extracted slowly. Our natural soaps cure for 21 to 28 days, and moringa seeds are dried in the sun before pressing.

Clean, not loud

We make clean skincare and explain what is in it. We do not call our products Ayurvedic or organic while those certifications are still in process.

A few products, each with a job

Nineteen products today, chosen so that a morning or evening ritual stays short. New ones arrive with the seasons.

Ingredient philosophy

Ingredients you already know.

Aloe, rose, moringa, turmeric, ghee. Made to be gentle for daily use; if your skin is very reactive, patch test first.
