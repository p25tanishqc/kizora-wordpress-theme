Hydrating

Aloe vera

Ghritkumari

The clear gel inside the leaves of the aloe plant, known in many Indian homes as ghritkumari.

Suits: Dryness, skin that feels tight or warm after sun or shaving.

Traditional use

Used at home for generations on minor sunburn and dry skin.

What the evidence says

The gel is mostly water with polysaccharides. Reviews describe moisturising and soothing properties; much of the research is laboratory or small studies, so it is best described as a light hydrator that feels cooling.

How it works on skin

Leaves a light layer that holds water on the skin surface; feels cool.

Who should be careful

Rare allergy; patch test first. It is a light hydrator, not a treatment for burns or acne.
