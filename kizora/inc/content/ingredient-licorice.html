Licorice root (mulethi)

Mulethi

Extract of licorice root, known in India as mulethi.

Suits: Dry lips.

Traditional use

Used in home remedies and Ayurvedic preparations.

What the evidence says

Some licorice compounds have been studied for uneven pigmentation, mostly in the lab. A lip-balm 'dark spot repair' claim would need product-level proof.

How it works on skin

Part of a ghee-based lip balm.

Who should be careful

Avoid 'repair' and 'brightening' claims unless tested.
