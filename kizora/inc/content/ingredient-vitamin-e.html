Vitamin E

A fat-soluble antioxidant; the acetate form is common in cosmetics.

Suits: Drier skin.

Traditional use

Not a traditional ingredient; a vitamin widely used in modern skin care.

What the evidence says

A dermatology review describes vitamin E as an antioxidant widely used in skin care; effects depend on form and amount.

How it works on skin

Adds antioxidant support to the formula and a softer feel.

Who should be careful

Allergic reactions are uncommon but possible.
