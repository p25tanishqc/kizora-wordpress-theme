Moringa seed oil

Sahjan, drumstick

Oil pressed from the seeds of the drumstick tree (sahjan). Our seeds are dried in the sun before they are pressed.

Suits: Dry skin, especially at night and in winter.

Traditional use

Drumstick is a familiar kitchen plant across India; the seed oil has a long history of cosmetic use.

What the evidence says

A light, stable oil, high in oleic acid according to commonly published compositions. It works as an emollient: it softens and smooths. Claims about elasticity or ageing are not established.

How it works on skin

Fills the gaps between surface skin cells so skin feels softer and less dry.

Who should be careful

Use 2 to 3 drops; oily or acne-prone skin may prefer less.
