Plant waters

Rose (Damask rose)

Gulab

Rose water is the fragrant water collected when rose petals are steam-distilled; rose oil is the concentrated oil.

Suits: Refreshing dry or tired-looking skin.

Traditional use

Gulab jal has long been used in Indian homes as a face freshener.

What the evidence says

Valued mainly for its scent and as a gentle, refreshing water. Pharmacological reviews cover many uses, mostly not on skin, so skin claims should stay modest.

How it works on skin

Adds water and a soft scent; makes a mist feel pleasant to use.

Who should be careful

Fragrance compounds can bother very sensitive skin.

At a glance

On our labels: Rosa Damascena Flower Water; Rose Essential Oil

Found in: 3 Kizora products
