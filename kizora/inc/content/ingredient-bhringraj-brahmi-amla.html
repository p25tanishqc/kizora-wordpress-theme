Bhringraj, brahmi and amla

Three herbs traditionally used in Indian hair oils.

Traditional use

Classic ingredients in home-made hair oils for scalp massage.

What the evidence says

Claims about hair growth or stopping hair fall need product-level clinical proof; present these oils as conditioning oils for a scalp-massage ritual.

How it works on skin

Oil + massage softens dry hair and scalp.

Who should be careful

Patch test; wash out after an hour or overnight.
